# Barbarian Custom Class - Rules Cyclopedia Style

Brug denne reference, når en spiller eller NPC bruger projektets custom Barbarian class. Den er en bordklar destillation af `Regelbøger/The Barbarian - Unchained Fury of the Wilds (2nd edition).md`; verificer altid præcise tabelværdier i kildefilen før endeligt svar.

## Autoritet og grænser

- Kilde: `Regelbøger/The Barbarian - Unchained Fury of the Wilds (2nd edition).md`.
- Brug kun denne klasse som et class-specific supplement til Rules Cyclopedia. Generelle regler for attack rolls, saving throws, combat rounds, movement, morale, reaction, encumbrance, weapon mastery, dominion og War Machine kommer stadig fra Rules Cyclopedia.
- Kits og Appendix House Rules er optional. Brug dem kun, hvis DM'en eksplicit har aktiveret dem.
- Ignorer marketing-/produkttekst efter class-materialet i Barbarian-kilden. Den starter omkring `EXPERIENCE THE OSR SPIRIT IN A NEW DIMENSION`.

## Klassechassis

- Prime requisite: Strength. STR 13-15 giver +5% XP; STR 16-18 giver +10% XP.
- Ability requirements: Strength 9+, Constitution 9+.
- Alignment: Any non-Lawful.
- Hit Dice: 1d8 pr. level til 9th level; +3 hp pr. level derefter.
- Weapons: any.
- Armor: Leather eller Chain Mail; shields permitted. Plate Mail eller tungere armor forbydes og negerer class abilities indtil fjernet.
- Attack progression: Fighter THAC0.
- Saving throws: Fighter saving throws.
- Extra attacks: ingen ekstra attacks fra level alene; ekstra attacks kommer fra Weapon Mastery, magic eller specifikke maneuvers.

## Rage procedure

1. Require visible enemy or immediate threat.
2. Start Rage as part of a normal melee or thrown attack.
3. Uses/day: level 1-3 = 1; level 4-7 = 2; level 8-11 = 3; level 12-14 = 4.
4. Duration: Barbarian level in rounds, minimum 3 rounds.
5. Apply while raging: +2 to hit and +2 damage with melee/thrown attacks; temporary hp equal to Barbarian level; +2 saves vs Death/Poison and Breath; AC worsens by +2; no Parry or calm-precision actions; no spellcasting, scrolls or wands.
6. Stacking: Rage stacks with magic weapons and Weapon Mastery. It does not stack with to-hit or damage bonuses from morale, fear or similar emotional states.
7. End: temporary hp vanish. No universal fatigue penalty in core Barbarian rules. The barbarian cannot Rage again for 1 turn.
8. Overuse: if the barbarian Rages more than twice in one hour/6 turns, save vs Paralysis/Petrify at the end of the second and subsequent Rages; failure means stunned for 1 round.
9. Optional Fatigue Variant is a house rule only: -2 to hit, AC worsens by +2, -2 saves for 1 turn; if Rage ended because no foe remained, 3 rounds instead.

## Class features

- Barbaric Danger Sense: if the barbarian leads in dungeon or wilderness, the party is surprised only on 1 on d6 unless foes are extraordinarily stealthy or magically concealed; barbarian gains +2 saves vs traps and natural hazards.
- Iron Constitution: +2 saves vs poison and disease at all times; stacks with Rage for +4 vs poison while raging. Natural healing gains +1 hp per full day of rest.
- Fleet of Foot: +10' encounter movement in leather/no armor and not beyond moderate encumbrance; +5' in chain. Overland movement gains +25% on foot in light gear.
- Wilderness Mastery: level-based Track, Forage, Hunt, Navigate and Hide in Wilderness checks. Open the source table before giving exact odds.
- Barbaric Intimidation: force a Reaction roll with +2 in barbarian's favor against creatures with HD <= barbarian level. It is not mind control and does not affect fear-immune creatures.
- Unrefined: optional; new barbarians begin illiterate in Common but speak Common and native tribal language. One downtime week plus tutor at 5 gp/day removes illiteracy.
- Heavy Armor Taboo: plate or heavier armor negates Rage, Danger Sense, Fleet of Foot and Hide in Wilderness until removed.

## Name level: tribe, warband og totem

- At 9th level, a barbarian may claim or found a Tribe functioning as stronghold/cultural dominion.
- Great Lodge/encampment options: Palisaded Camp, Timber Lodge, Stone Hall. Verify cost, build time, upkeep and population in source.
- Followers: monthly 1d6 x 5 0-level tribesmen; doubles add a named champion level 3-5 barbarian.
- Warband capacity: up to 20% of home population.
- Totem: while on home soil and carrying the totem sign, followers gain +1 Morale and the chieftain gains +1 Reaction with allied tribes and totem-linked beasts.
- Dominion turns: use normal Rules Cyclopedia dominion procedures plus the Barbarian tribe procedures in source lines 405-449.

## Optional kits

Only use with explicit DM approval. Each kit keeps the core chassis unchanged and adds trade-offs.

- Titan of Two-Hands: great-weapon berserker.
- Shield-Wall Reaver: defensive shield fighter.
- Horse-Lord Raider: mounted raider.

Open the source before applying benefits, restrictions, mounted maneuvers, shield-wall effects or kit-specific trade-offs.
